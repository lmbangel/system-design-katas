Study plan · October 2026 – September 2027

Four exams, one architect.

The architecture track as a working roadmap: every week is one decision to understand, prove in Azure and explain. Ten hours a week: five on the exam track, three mastering the guide's concepts, two on coding. Tick weeks off as you go; the current week opens itself, and the exams count down. Your week

10 hours: 5 on the exam track, 3 on the guide's concepts, 2 on coding.

Day Hours Block What you do

Concept order
The learning map's architect path, two concepts a week. Concepts that repeat each other count as one session (idempotency appears on 01, 03 and 04). When a sprint covers the same ground, take the concepts from its page: 07 during Sprint 2, 08 during Sprint 3's data weeks.
Exam weeks
The last week of a sprint flips: seven hours of practice tests and weak areas, one hour reviewing concepts, coding stays at two.
Once a month
Sunday becomes a mock interview: whiteboard one applied design in 35 minutes, out loud, on a timer, and write down what you could not explain.
Mastered means
Level 3 on the map: explained without notes, you know when not to use it, and you have done the exercise. Reading is not mastering.
Missed a session
Drop the concept, never the coding or the exam spike: the exam dates and the coding habit are what compound.
